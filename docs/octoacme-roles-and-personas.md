# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Leads

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define the test strategy and ensure releases meet agreed quality and acceptance standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test plans, quality standards, and release criteria
- Coordinate functional, integration, regression, and exploratory testing
- Validate acceptance criteria and report defects and quality risks
- Guide test automation and improve quality practices across the delivery lifecycle

### Key Interactions
- Partner with Developers on testability, defect resolution, and automated test coverage
- Collaborate with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria and prioritize defects
- Report quality metrics, release readiness, and unresolved risks to Project Managers

### Success Criteria
- Acceptance and release criteria are validated before deployment
- Critical defects are identified early and resolved or explicitly accepted
- Quality trends and release risks are visible to the delivery team

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide solution design and technical decisions so systems remain secure, scalable, and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and document significant architectural decisions
- Establish engineering standards and guide implementation approaches
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and debt
- Evaluate solution feasibility and long-term operational impact

### Key Interactions
- Advise Developers through design discussions, implementation guidance, and code reviews
- Review feasibility and technical trade-offs with Product Managers
- Communicate technical dependencies, risks, and resource needs to Project Managers

### Success Criteria
- Solutions satisfy product needs and agreed architectural standards
- Technical risks and dependencies are identified and addressed early
- Technical debt is visible, prioritized, and managed

---

## Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate effective team practices, remove delivery blockers, and help teams continuously improve.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Surface and help remove process and delivery blockers
- Coach the team on agile principles and sustainable ways of working
- Track and support improvements identified through team feedback

### Key Interactions
- Support Project Managers with delivery processes, dependency visibility, and escalation
- Facilitate Developer collaboration and help resolve workflow impediments
- Coach Product Managers on backlog refinement, prioritization, and iterative delivery

### Success Criteria
- Ceremonies produce clear decisions, owners, and next steps
- Blockers are surfaced promptly and resolved or escalated
- Team flow, predictability, and continuous improvement strengthen over time

---

## Stakeholder Representatives

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives from Sales, Support, Leadership, and other functions provide customer, market, operational, and strategic perspectives throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, market context, operational needs, and organizational priorities
- Validate product assumptions and provide timely input or approvals
- Communicate project decisions and launch plans within their functions
- Support product adoption and report post-release feedback

### Key Interactions
- Provide Product Managers with evidence and feedback for product decisions
- Coordinate timing, dependencies, and organizational impacts with Project Managers
- Consult with Developers on customer scenarios and operational constraints

### Success Criteria
- Product decisions reflect relevant customer and organizational needs
- Required feedback and approvals arrive in time to support delivery
- Functional teams are prepared for launch, adoption, and ongoing support
